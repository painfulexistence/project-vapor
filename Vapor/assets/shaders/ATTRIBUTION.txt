Third-party shader attribution
===============================

VHS effect (3d_post_process.metal / PostProcess.frag)
-----------------------------------------------------
The VHS post-processing effect (screen curvature, tracking wobble, and the
scrolling glitch/dropout stripes) is derived from Ryk's "VCR distortion"
shader.
